# Data

## Primary dataset (included)

`TB-File-01.csv` is the publicly available dataset of Beltrame, Amelard, Wong & Hughson (2017), *Prediction of
oxygen uptake dynamics by machine learning analysis of wearable sensors during activities of daily living*,
Scientific Reports 7:45738, https://doi.org/10.1038/srep45738, redistributed under its CC BY 4.0 licence.
16 participants; wearable signals and breath-by-breath V̇O₂ interpolated to 1 Hz by the original authors.
The original data collection was approved by the Office of Research Ethics at the University of Waterloo (ORE 20931).
